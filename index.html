Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. About this service

This is a privately operated, self-hosted Immich photo and video management service for personal and family use. Access is limited to invited family members and the server administrator.

2. Information we process

The service processes photos and videos, including associated metadata, that users upload or that are made available through the external photo library.

When users sign in with Google, the service receives basic authentication information such as their email address and profile information, according to the permissions granted during Google sign-in.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to provide photo and video management, user authentication, and access to the private family library.

4. Storage and security

Photos and videos are stored on privately operated server hardware. The external photo archive is mounted read-only. Access to the service is restricted to authorized accounts.

5. Google sign-in

Google is used only for authentication. Google sign-in allows the service to verify a user's identity and obtain basic profile information. Photos and videos are not stored in Google Drive by this service.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Sharing

Personal photos and videos are intended for private use by authorized family members. They are not sold or used for advertising.

7. Data retention and deletion

Information is retained while the relevant account or media remains on the service. Users can contact the administrator to request account or data deletion.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the server administrator at: clentyx@gmail.com
